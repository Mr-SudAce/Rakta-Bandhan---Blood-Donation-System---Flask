Our Story

Saving Lives, One Drop at a Time

RaktaBandhan is a tech-driven life-saving network dedicated to connecting donors with patients across Nepal in real-time.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between donors and recipients, ensuring no life is lost due to blood shortages.

Our Community

Building a compassionate network of volunteers and health organizations working together.

Our Promise

To provide a secure, reliable, and lightning-fast platform for emergency blood requests.

Who We Are

RaktaBandhan was born out of a simple idea:
Technology can save lives.

We realized that while there are many willing donors, the connection between them and recipients is often delayed. Our platform solves this by providing real-time donor tracking and instant request fulfillment.
